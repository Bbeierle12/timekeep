# Project Brief

## What is Timekeep?

Timekeep is a full-stack timekeeping and compliance platform designed specifically for California labor law compliance, focusing on meal and rest break rules.

## Problem Statement

California has strict labor laws requiring employers to provide meal and rest breaks at specific intervals. Non-compliance results in premium pay penalties. Businesses need a reliable system to:
- Track employee clock-in/out times accurately
- Monitor meal and rest break compliance
- Generate compliance reports
- Alert managers to potential violations before they occur

## Core Objectives

1. **Accurate Time Tracking** - Provide employees with easy clock-in/out via web and mobile
2. **California Compliance** - Enforce meal break rules, rest break rules, overtime calculations
3. **Real-time Alerts** - Warn managers and employees before violations occur
4. **Audit Trail** - Maintain complete records for labor board inquiries
5. **Payroll Integration** - Generate reports compatible with payroll systems

## Target Users

- **Employees**: Clock in/out, certify time entries, view history
- **Admins/Managers**: Review time entries, manage employees, run reports
- **Owners**: Configure company settings, compliance rules, view dashboards

## Success Criteria

- Zero missed meal break alerts
- 100% time entry certification before payroll
- Complete audit trail for all changes
- Mobile-first employee experience
- Sub-second response times
